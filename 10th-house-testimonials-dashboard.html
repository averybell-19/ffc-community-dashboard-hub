Quick take — 2026 only

  • Members join for connections — half of Spring '26 survey respondents named "connections & network" as the single most valuable thing they got out of membership.
  • Biggest recurring complaint: a quiet, self-promotional feed with low peer engagement (staff replying more than members) — shows up across multiple low-score responses.
  • Biggest churn driver: no time/capacity, followed by no reason given and budget — not dissatisfaction with content.
  • Operational gap: billing/auto-renew confusion is costing refunds and goodwill — see flag below.

    How this is built

    For each of the top 9 tracked industries, the members with the highest combined engagement score (event attendance + feedback volume + rating + tenure, pulled from the Event Member Activity leaderboards) are surfaced as that industry's "go-tos" — plus, where we have one on file, a direct quote from a member in that industry about the community. Think of this as the people a prospective member in that industry could be pointed to. Hospitality & Event Planning isn't shown — none of its members currently rank on an engagement leaderboard.
