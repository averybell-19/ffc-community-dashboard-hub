What's changing

AI adoption is visibly entering member workflows — website building, agentic brand systems, SEO/search shifts. This wasn't a visible thread a few months ago.

Members are reframing "visibility" from a marketing/content problem to a support/community problem.

Staff-run summer engagement campaigns (Founder Fuel, CEO Club) are driving some of the highest comment counts in recent history.

Linked posts: Visibility as support Founder Fuel #3 CEO Club giveaway

Objections we're hearing

Not Paid (billing/payment failure) is the single largest cancellation reason — ~35% of a ~500-record sample. That's a dunning problem, not a dissatisfaction signal.

"No longer a fit" (~24%) and "financial reasons" (~9%) are the top qualitative objections. Smaller but direct: "outgrown content/community" and "product/service unsatisfactory."

Caveat: 34 of the last 43 cancellations (79%) have no reason logged — a data-capture gap worth closing. Source: HubSpot subscription cancellation-reason field (not Circle — no post links here).

What Marketing should know

"Visibility is infrastructure, not vanity" and "support problem, not content problem" threads pull strong engagement — lean messaging into peer support over content polish.

Giveaway/challenge formats work: Founder Fuel posts pull 5-11 comments vs. a typical 0-3.

Linked posts: Visibility post Founder Fuel #2

What Programming should know

Fashion and Beauty & Personal Care remain the most active industry cohorts by conversation volume (consistent with all-time tags: Beauty & Personal Care 70, Health & Wellness 59, Fashion & Accessories 39).

Recurring unmet needs: trademark/legal, packaging/MOQ sourcing, 3PL/fulfillment, fractional CMO/CFO.

Linked posts: Next Fashion Huddle 3PL question

What Growth should know

"Ask the Community" is the dominant space — 36 of 87 recent posts (41%). Organic peer-to-peer activity is healthy and worth protecting.

Regional chapters keep generating real meetup requests — a growth lever beyond national programming.

Linked posts: Ask the Community NY/Tri-State chapter
