🗣️ What the community is talking about

Top 5 themes members are talking about

  1. Vendor & service-provider referrals (packaging/MOQ, manufacturing, 3PL, legal, PR, dev, trademark)
  2. Partnership & collab requests (brand collabs, swag bags, event co-hosting)
  3. Founder visibility & mindset (self-promotion discomfort, burnout, community-as-support)
  4. AI tools entering daily workflows (site-building, agentic brand systems, AI search shift) — still early/low-volume, worth watching rather than acting on yet
  5. Fundraising & alternative capital (crowdfunding, angel investment, creator equity)
Linked posts: Factory recco needed Trademark attorney recco Collab request Building agentically Fundraising post

What's changing

  • AI adoption is visibly entering member workflows — website building, agentic brand systems, SEO/search shifts. This wasn't a visible thread a few months ago.
  • Members are reframing "visibility" from a marketing/content problem to a support/community problem.
  • Staff-run summer engagement campaigns (Founder Fuel, CEO Club) are driving some of the highest comment counts in recent history.
  • Newly flagged: the single highest-engagement post this cycle wasn't a staff campaign — it's an organic peer "let's grow our Instagrams together" thread (16 comments). Peer-to-peer growth support is resonating more than any top-down content right now.
Linked posts: Visibility as support Founder Fuel #3 CEO Club giveaway "My Instagram is stuck"

Objections we're hearing

  • Not Paid (billing/payment failure) is the single largest cancellation reason — ~37.5% of a ~500-record sample. That's a dunning problem, not a dissatisfaction signal.
  • "No longer a fit" (~19-24%, drifting down) and "financial reasons" (~9%) are the top qualitative objections.
  • Smaller but direct: "outgrown content/community" and "product/service unsatisfactory."

Caveat: 34 of the last 43 cancellations (79%) have no reason logged — a data-capture gap worth closing. Source: HubSpot subscription cancellation-reason field (not Circle — no post links here).

🧭 What each team should take away

What Marketing should know

  • "Visibility is infrastructure, not vanity" — the reframe members are actually using in conversation this month. Steal it for copy.
  • Peer conversation beats produced content: an organic member thread on feeling stuck growing their following pulled more real engagement (16 comments) than anything staff posted — people are talking to each other about this, not waiting for our content.
  • Content cue: lean the next campaign into peer stories and real member exchanges, not more "expert tips."
Evidence: Visibility post Instagram growth thread

What Programming should know

  • Fashion + Beauty & Personal Care are our most engaged communities — program there first, every time budget is tight.
  • The #1 unmet need isn't content, it's operators: legal/trademark, sourcing, fulfillment, and fractional exec support keep coming up across every industry.
  • Big swing: a recurring "Ops Clinic" would answer a demand that's been showing up for months, not just this cycle.
Evidence: Trademark attorney recco 3PL question

What Growth should know

  • Organic beats programmed: "Ask the Community" out-drives every campaign we run — 36 of 86 recent posts (42%).
  • Members are growing us themselves: regional chapters keep generating real, self-initiated meetup demand — a growth lever we're not fully funding yet.
  • Move: protect the organic engine, and put real budget behind chapter organizers before adding another national program.
Evidence: Ask the Community NY/Tri-State chapter
